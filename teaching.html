Teaching

Since 2020 I have taught mobile robotics, IoT and embedded systems at Macquarie University, with one aim: make autonomous systems approachable, and give students the confidence to build things that move. I also mentor student teams in the MQ Robotics Club, Drone Society and MARS Rover, and supervise honours and masters theses.
